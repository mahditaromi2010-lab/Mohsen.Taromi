Hiring an overseas truck driver through a DAMA

A short, plain guide for employers in the Northern Territory and Western Australia.

What a DAMA is

A Designated Area Migration Agreement lets employers in a regional area sponsor overseas workers on terms that standard visas do not offer, such as concessions on age (under 55) and a lower English requirement.

How it works in five steps

  1. 1

    Show the need

    Demonstrate that you cannot fill the truck driver role locally.

  2. 2

    Get endorsed

    The region's Designated Area Representative endorses you for Truck Driver (General), ANZSCO 733111.

  3. 3

    Sign a labour agreement

    Agree terms with the Department of Home Affairs.

  4. 4

    Nominate the driver

    Nominate a named worker for the role.

  5. 5

    Visa and arrival

    The driver applies for the visa and travels to you.
