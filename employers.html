Hiring an overseas truck driver through a DAMA

Short on drivers in the NT or regional WA? Here is how a transport business can sponsor an experienced driver like Mohsen, in plain English.

What a DAMA is

A Designated Area Migration Agreement lets businesses in certain regions sponsor overseas workers for jobs they cannot fill locally, including truck drivers. It can allow concessions that standard visas do not, such as a higher age limit (under 55 for eligible occupations) and a lower English requirement.

How sponsorship works

  1. Show you need a driverYour business is in a DAMA region and has tried to hire locally first.
  2. Get endorsed by the regionApply to the region's Designated Area Representative (contacts below) for endorsement to sponsor a Truck Driver (General), ANZSCO 733111.
  3. Sign a labour agreementWith the endorsement, request a DAMA labour agreement from the Department of Home Affairs.
  4. Nominate the driverNominate Mohsen for the truck driver position under that agreement.
  5. Visa and arrivalMohsen applies for the employer-sponsored visa and completes his licence conversion and assessments.
